Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you respond to a quote request?

We typically respond to quote requests within 24 hours during business hours. For urgent requests, please call us directly at +27 (0)81 340 7183.

Do you operate outside South Africa?

Yes! We operate across South Africa and are expanding into SADC countries including Mozambique, Botswana, and Namibia. Contact us to discuss cross-border requirements.

Are your services OHS compliant?

Absolutely. All our operations meet strict occupational health and safety standards. Compliance is non-negotiable across all our services.

Yes, we have 24/7 emergency logistics support. Call our emergency hotline for immediate assistance with urgent transport needs.

What makes Kuhlula Legacy different?

Our integrated approach means you get logistics, maintenance, and construction expertise under one roof—reducing complexity and improving coordination for your projects.

Yes, we specialize in long-term service contracts. We can customize arrangements for logistics, maintenance, and construction projects of any duration.
