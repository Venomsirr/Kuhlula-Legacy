About Kuhlula Legacy

Transport, supply and project services from Kempton Park, South Africa.

Who We Are

A Black-owned South African business supporting transport, supply and construction needs

Kuhlula Legacy supports businesses and projects with road freight transportation, supply and delivery of general goods, shuttle services, plant hire and construction. We are committed to dependable service and meaningful economic participation.

Our integrated approach brings transport, supply, equipment hire and construction capabilities under one trusted partnership, helping clients coordinate their project and operational needs.

Based in Kempton Park, Gauteng, we work with clients to understand their service requirements and project needs.

Our Vision

To be a trusted partner for transport, supply and project services in South Africa.

Our Mission

To deliver road freight, goods supply, shuttle services, plant hire and construction projects with a strong commitment to safety, integrity and quality, while creating sustainable economic opportunities.
